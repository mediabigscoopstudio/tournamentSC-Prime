How can we help?

Frequently asked questions

My registration isn't showing up

Registrations can take a few minutes to appear after payment/approval. If it's still missing after an hour, raise a "Registration not showing" ticket.

I've been put in the wrong team or bracket

Raise a "Wrong team assignment" or "Wrong opponent or bracket" ticket with the tournament name — the organizer will be asked to correct it.

A match score or result looks wrong

Use "Incorrect match score" or "Match result not updated" so we can flag it to the organizer running that fixture.

Live score isn't updating

Try refreshing the page first — live scores poll automatically. If it's stuck, raise a "Live score not updating" ticket with the match link.

I'm not getting notifications

Check your spam folder, then raise a "Notification not received" ticket so we can check delivery on our end.

I have a payment or fee question

Raise a "Payment/registration fee issue" ticket with the tournament name and we'll look into it.

Still stuck? You can always email us directly at {{ support_email }}.
