How can we help?

Frequently asked questions

I'm having trouble creating or setting up a tournament

Raise a "Tournament creation issue" or "Tournament setup issue" ticket with the tournament name and what step failed.

Registrations, players or teams aren't behaving as expected

Use "Registration management", "Player management" or "Team management" depending on what's wrong.

Fixtures, brackets or match assignments are wrong

Raise a "Fixture scheduling issue", "Bracket management" or "Match assignment issue" ticket with round/match details.

Scores, live updates or standings are off

Use "Score/result management", "Live score issue" or "Tournament standings issue" and we'll take a look.

I need to cancel or postpone a tournament

Raise a "Tournament cancellation or postponement" ticket so we can help coordinate with entrants.

I have a payout or venue question

Raise a "Payment/payout issue" or "Venue management" ticket with the relevant tournament.

Still stuck? You can always email us directly at {{ support_email }}.
